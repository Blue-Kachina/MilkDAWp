# MilkDAWp user guide

MilkDAWp is a music visualizer. It plays MilkDrop presets, rendered by
[projectM](https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm), that change in
time with your music. It comes two ways:

- **a plugin** (VST3 on Windows, macOS and Linux; Audio Unit on macOS) that
  you put on a track in your DAW. It listens to that track and follows the
  DAW's tempo and transport;
- **a standalone app** that listens to an audio input, or on Windows to
  whatever your computer is playing.

Both come with about 9,800 presets ("Cream of the Crop", curated by Jason
Fletcher) and share your ratings, favourites and tags.
